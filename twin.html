MODEL NOTES

Simulated physics: PV power = 20 W × G/1000 × soiling × (1 − 0.4 %/°C × (T − 25)); panel heating from a thermal-mass balance; wiping, spray, battery and energy use are modelled. Geometry is illustrative, not manufacturing CAD. Values are not measurements.

Forecast: a recency-weighted trend is fitted to recent simulated Panel A temperature readings and projected 10 simulated minutes ahead, damped towards the thermal equilibrium. Alerts compare that projection with the 45 °C cooling trigger and the 0.90 soiling-index cleaning trigger.
